# YT-DL Bar

YT-DL Bar is a macOS 27 menu bar app for saving a video or its audio from a link. The download is done by yt-dlp.

Click the down arrow in the menu bar. Paste a link, choose Video or Audio, and download. A link that also belongs to a playlist saves that one video only.

Right-click Video or Audio for its other formats and for quick settings. Right-click the same button again to close that drawer. Right-click the menu bar icon to change the theme and to hide controls.

Video saves an MP4. Audio saves an MP3 unless you pick M4A. Discord keeps a video under 20 MB.

The app does not show a Dock icon.
